Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Saint may process information when you install or use the application.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server, Saint may process Discord-provided identifiers and community data needed to operate commands and configured features, such as server identifiers, user identifiers, roles, channels, messages or moderation-related events.

3. Why We Process Data

Data is used to provide requested bot functionality, enforce configured moderation systems, maintain settings, troubleshoot problems and improve reliability.

4. Data Retention

Retention depends on the feature and configuration. Data that is no longer required for an active feature should be removed according to the application's operational practices.

5. Third Parties

Saint operates through Discord's platform and may rely on infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service. Saint does not sell personal information.

6. Your Choices

Server administrators can remove Saint from a server to stop server-level functionality. Where a feature stores configurable data, administrators can request assistance regarding that data through the support contact.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Saint support address provided on the official website.

This policy should be reviewed and updated to match Saint's actual data storage, hosting providers and features before production use or Discord verification.
